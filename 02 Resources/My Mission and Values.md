# My-Mission-Values

I want to raise Elias and Eliana to be good people. 

I don't want my family to feel like they are competing for me with my work pursuits (while it is important that I balance this with real negotiating of real interests and needs; it should not be adversarial)  

I want to be on other people's team. I want to share their burdens and their joys

## [[Researcher]]  

I am fascinated by discovery and design. I feel most alive and curious when I'm in a library, or looking at a new discovery, or reading about an invention, or watching someone create. How do humans make important new discoveries about the world, or design effective new solutions to hard problems they care about? What configurations of information, technology and people can (more) reliably give rise to more discovery and design? And how can we ensure that these configurations are open and sustainable?  

I want to answer these questions in a deep and enduring way. I want my answers to not break down in the real world. I want them to be as timeless as possible, and if they have to change, to provide a useful foundation for future work to build on. In other words, I want the field to be better off for my contributions.   

I think especially of [this piece on cargo cult science](https://meaningness.com/metablog/upgrade-your-cargo-cult) and of this quote in "on bullshit":  

> In the elder days of Art, <br/>Builders wrought with greatest care<br/>Each minute and unseen part; <br/>For the Gods see everywhere.   

This means that I want my work to _not_ be cargo cult or bullshit: I want it to be **real**. I don't want to hand wave and play the game of publishing papers and winning grants. I want everything we do to be for real. Our goal is not to satisfy reviewers, the press, or our peers, but to satisfy Truth, insofar as its standards can be represented by others. This means also that I am continually monitoring and upgrading (when necessary) the community of practice I am a part of (including its epistemic norms and practices).   ^8338jt

At an impact level, I am interested in configurations of information, technology, and people that are *just*: I want to know if we can build configurations that are equitable and not dependent on privilege and Matthew Effects that are resistant to newcomers and outsiders and people with less resources to start with. I would be very sad if the only people who benefit from what I discover and design are those who are already privileged or mostly interested in making \$\$. I want to empower learners, communities, and governments to make the world a better place.  

Therefore, my work should be characterized by care and patience. This does not mean inactivity or sloth, or that I mistake fear for care. Working out this balance for myself will be a lifelong project.   ^h25he2

Concretely, this looks like:   
- Regularly reading, recording, and sharing insights from relevant literatures.  
- Regularly sharing works-in-progress at a variety of outlets: seminars, preprints, blogposts, etc.  
- I am particularly interested in cultivating a Digital Garden  
- Regularly tending to / cultivating my epistemic toolkit.  
- Regular practices that help me balance consumption and production.  

## [[Mentor and Teacher]]

Also part of my vocation is a mentor. To students, and those around me. I want to [teach the students I have](https://www.chronicle.com/article/Teaching-the-Students-We-Have/245290), not the ones I wish I had. I want to always view them as people, with lives and goals, not means or obstacles to an end.  

I want all of my mentees to have the tools, space, and training they need to be achieve their goals, whether this means being a thriving independent researcher, or not.   ^pse8ow

Part of what this means is to wield my power and authority appropriately. Not shy away from it altogether (which I have a strong tendency to do), but also not use it to dominate. This means getting better and bolder at setting clear expectations, and giving clear and honest feedback when standards are not met, but also pair that with compassionate suggestions and help to get back on track.   ^95tv9x

## [[Steward and (Academic) Citizen]]

This really resonates with me. For the longest time I have been sort of like a ravager: taking resources and discarding them when they are depleted. I have been very bad at maintaining things, caring for what I own, optimizing them and making sure they are in good working order, fixing things that are broken, etc.  
I want my life to look different. 

I want to have deliberate, regular, yet natural, practices that tend to / cultivate my body (rest, eating well, being active), my relationships (family, friends, community), and my responsibilities (e.g., home, workspace, lab).   ^t6r85u

A foundation for this too is a regular practice of [negotiating my needs](negotiating needs). I do not want to be overcommitted. ==I want to whole-ass every thing I choose to say yes to.==^s0btuo

This role includes my service role as an academic. This is my neighborhood, and I want to make sure I think of it that way: there must be some sense of responsibility and ownership for any problems that arise. I want to be mindful of my resources/limitations in expending effort towards solving those problems, but I at least want to think of things that way.
